Competitive Pokemon is a passion of mine, think of chess combined with a 17 way game of rock, paper sissors. The skill floor for basic play is very low but the skill ceiling can be infinite. With this being a very roll heavy game each game will play out differently every game

Subheading

Competitive is played in two distinct formats, Offical doubles format which is ran by the games devolpers Gamefreak or Smogon singles a metagame designed and ran by the online community of the same name. Those tiers being, OU, UU, RU, NU, and PU
